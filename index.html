FAQ

Get in touch

Why are you more expensive than a freelancer?

We sit in the sweet spot. Freelancers can be unreliable and often lack the full skill set (design + dev + strategy). We provide agency-level quality and reliability without the agency-level overhead costs. You pay for senior talent and results, not for our office rent.

Is there a long-term contract for subscriptions?

No. You can pause or cancel anytime. If you don't have work for us next month, just pause your subscription and resume when you do. We believe we should earn your business every single month.

What is the average delivery time?

For subscriptions, most design tasks are delivered in 24-48 hours. Complex dev tasks take 2-4 days. For fixed projects: Landing Pages (2-3 weeks), Corporate Websites (4-6 weeks), E-commerce (6-8 weeks). We prioritize speed without sacrificing quality.

Who owns the intellectual property?

You do. 100%. Once the invoice is paid, all code, designs, and assets belong to you. We don't hold your business hostage. We provide a full handover of files and credentials upon completion.

Do you provide hosting and domain?

We can handle it for you via our "Web Care" plan. If you prefer to manage it yourself, we will guide you on the best providers (we recommend Vercel for frontend and AWS/DigitalOcean for backend) and help you with the setup.

Do you work with templates?

No. We build custom solutions tailored to your specific business goals. Templates are bloated, slow, and hard to scale. We build assets that are meant to last and perform.
